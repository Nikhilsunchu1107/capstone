### q30 · C_apply_rule
**Q:** A person with a new Arogya Sanjeevani policy is hospitalised for an illness 20 days after the first policy commencement date. Are the expenses covered?

**Gold:** No. Expenses for any illness within 30 days from the first policy commencement date are excluded, except claims arising from an accident. The exclusion does not apply if the insured has continuous coverage for more than twelve months.

- `Arogya_Sanjeevani_Policy_Wording_KEN_034` p5: "Expenses related to the treatment of any illness within 30 days from the first policy commencement date shall be excluded except claims arising due to an accident ... shall not, however, apply if the Insured Person has Continuous Coverage for more than twelve months"

### q31 · C_apply_rule
**Q:** Under a new SBI General Livestock policy, an insured animal dies of a disease contracted 10 days after the commencement of risk. Is the death covered?

**Gold:** No. Death due to diseases contracted within 15 days from the date of commencement of risk is excluded, unless the insurance is a continuous renewal without a break or the Waiver of Waiting Period add-on (4.4) is opted.

- `SBI_General_Livestock_Policy_Wording_38e` p2: "Death of insured Animal due to diseases contracted within 15 days from the Date of Commencement of Risk, unless such insurance is a continuous renewal ... or Add-On cover 4.4, Waiver of Waiting Period, is opted"

### q33 · C_apply_rule
**Q:** Under the Contractors Plant and Machinery Insurance policy, the Company receives the notice and completed claim form 20 days after the damage occurred. Is the Company liable?
